## Robert Winer, M.D., Dipl. (FAZ, Zürich), FRSM

**Credentials**

- Board-certified neurologist (Diplomate, American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology)
- Fellowship-trained psychopharmacologist
- University of Pennsylvania (Ivy League)
- National Hospital of Nervous Diseases, London
- Zentrum für Tiefenpsychologie, Zürich — Diplomate (FAZ)
- Fellow of the Royal Society of Medicine (FRSM)
- Former Medical School Professor

---

**Current practice is restricted to:**

- Psychoanalysis
- Single (Second Opinion) Examinations in Neurology, Neuropsychiatry (with or without testing), and Psychopharmacology

**Not offered:**

- Medications prescribed
- Ongoing treatment of patients under the age of majority (18)
- Family and couples evaluations and treatment

All services are performed personally.
