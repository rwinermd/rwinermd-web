**About the practice:**

Dr. Winer's psychoanalytic practice draws on Jungian and psychodynamic traditions, with training at the Zentrum für Tiefenpsychologie in Zürich (Diplomate, FAZ) and decades of clinical experience. The current practice is restricted to adult patients (18 and over) seen individually.

Six-session introductory packages are available for new patients at $2,400 (sliding scale).
