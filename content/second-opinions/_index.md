## Why a Second Opinion?

To gain access, accommodation, and ongoing care in neurology, medicine, and mental health — including issues relating to the ADA, private disability, and Social Security/Medicare.

A second opinion can help with:

- **Treatment options** — finding alternatives when current treatment isn't working
- **Diagnostic options** — getting clarity when doctors disagree or are uncertain
- **New, novel, or experimental treatments**

---

## When to Consider a Second Opinion

**For family members:**

- When a loved one in high school, college, or later life has an acute or chronic condition
- On matters of aging and chronic care

**For yourself:**

- Doctors don't agree on your diagnosis, or have offered multiple diagnoses
- You sense their uncertainty or discomfort in not knowing
- You still feel terrible and don't know where to turn, or which treatment or operation is best
- You've spent thousands of dollars on health and mental health care, felt better with each new treatment, and now symptoms have returned
